Inside our Rue du Rhône atelier, a single master watchmaker devotes up to four hundred hours to each movement. Every bridge bevel is hand-burnished with gentian wood; every screw head is mirror-polished until light vanishes into pure reflection.

We reject mass automation in favor of tactile permanence. By uniting Grade 5 titanium’s featherweight resilience with the warm density of 18k gold, VELTOR builds instruments that honor traditional Swiss chronometry while advancing modern material engineering.

How long does delivery take?

We offer complimentary private courier delivery worldwide. Depending on your location, delivery takes 3-5 business days.

What is the warranty policy?

Every VELTOR timepiece comes with a lifetime guarantee on the movement and a 5-year international warranty on all other components.

Can I return a watch if I change my mind?

Yes, we offer a 14-day return policy for unworn timepieces in their original packaging, provided the protective seals are intact.
